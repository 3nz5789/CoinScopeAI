3. Product Architecture

3.1 Core Engine: Deterministic AI Stack

Hidden Markov Model (HMM) Regime Scanner

  • Baum-Welch algorithm for unsupervised regime detection
  • T-20s historical volatility analysis
  • Four states: TRENDING (mint), VOLATILE (amber), MEAN-REVERTING (slate), QUIET (deep slate)
  • Regime-aware signal weighting: confluence scores adjusted by current state probability

Multi-Factor Signal Scoring

  • Six-datum signal console: momentum, volume, funding rate, order-book imbalance, on-chain flow, sentiment
  • Each factor scored 0-100; weighted confluence determines action level
  • Action chips: EXECUTE (emerald), CONSIDER (amber), SKIP (muted), HALT (crimson)

Fractional Kelly Criterion Risk Gate

  • Full Kelly: f* = (p*b - q) / b
  • Enforced half-Kelly for capital preservation
  • Heat gauge: current position size / max allowed by gate
  • Kill switch: manual ARMED/DISARMED with journal requirement

Telegram Integration: @ScoopyAI_bot

  • Real-time signal alerts with regime context
  • Position sizing recommendations
  • Daily settlement and P&L summaries
  • No autonomous execution — operator maintains final authority

7. Risk Factors & Mitigation

Regulatory Risk

Risk: Jordan Law No. 14 and similar bans restrict crypto asset access.
Mitigation: Multi-jurisdictional structure insulates from single-market shocks. VARA and DASP licenses provide institutional credibility.

Market Risk

Risk: Crypto winter reduces trading volume and subscription willingness.
Mitigation: HMM classifier performs best in volatile markets. 1956 Fund provides uncorrelated SME yield. Freemium tier maintains user base during downturns.

Technical Risk

Risk: HMM model degradation or Kelly Criterion misapplication causes user losses.
Mitigation: Platform is signal intelligence only — no autonomous execution. Kill switch requires written journal entry. Backtesting framework with 5-year historical validation.
