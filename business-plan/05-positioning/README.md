## Folder purpose

This folder answers four questions that, once answered, become the source of truth for every external surface:

1. **What is CoinScopeAI?** A locked positioning statement, plus a short version and a one-sentence version.
2. **What category does it claim?** A documented decision among the four candidate categories (AI trading intelligence platform / automated crypto futures system / trader operating system / institutional-grade signal/risk platform), with rationale.
3. **How does it differentiate?** Against generic trading tools, signal groups, simple scanners, and black-box automation narratives.
4. **What messaging is approved across surfaces?** A master message, supporting messages, proof points, trust language, and claims guardrails — usable on the website, in sales conversations, in onboarding copy, and in content.

The result is the **operator-grade positioning lock**. Where folder `04` answered who buys, this folder answers what they read, in what order, in what voice, and what we will not say.

## Why this folder matters

Three failure modes this folder exists to prevent:

- **Category drift under acquisition pressure.** The four candidate categories imply different ICP, pricing, GTM, and trust posture. Without a documented lock, founder-led conversations and content drift across categories week to week, and the brand becomes incoherent. Investors notice. Buyers notice.
- **Marketing copy that breaks anti-overclaim discipline.** The trust premium is the moat. Specific phrases (`production-ready`, `guaranteed`, `10x your account`, `institutional-grade` without evidence, `best-in-class`) erode it on first contact. This folder formalizes the guardrails so every external surface inherits the same constraints.
- **Differentiation that lives in the founder's head.** A founder can describe how CoinScopeAI differs from a signal group in a 1-on-1 conversation; a website cannot. This folder is what the website inherits.
