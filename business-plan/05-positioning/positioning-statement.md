## 1. Primary positioning statement (long form)

> **For disciplined retail crypto-perp traders and small portfolio managers who have built their own risk frameworks** — and find that the tools available either replace those frameworks or ignore them — **CoinScopeAI is an AI-driven trader operating system that enforces the discipline you've already built.**
>
> Unlike signal services, autonomous bots, or exchange-native interfaces, CoinScopeAI runs configurable risk gates (drawdown, daily loss, leverage, position heat, max open positions) at user-defined thresholds *before* trades arm, classifies market regime in real time (Trending / Mean-Reverting / Volatile / Quiet) with confidence and gate-result on every signal, and keeps capital in the user's exchange account.
>
> CoinScopeAI is built and operated from the UAE, custody-free by structural choice, and gated against real-capital deployment until our Production Candidate Criteria pass. We do not generate alpha, deliver signals, custody capital, or execute autonomously without authorization.

This is the **complete** statement, suitable for an about page, a fundraising deck, an investor leave-behind, or a recruiting page. It includes audience, problem, distinction, evidence, posture, and explicit non-claims.

## 6. Why it is distinct (operator-grade)

Five distinctions, in order of how strongly each holds up under scrutiny. The full differentiation framework lives in `differentiation-framework.md`.

1. **Risk gates run *before* trade arming, not as a post-hoc audit.** Drawdown, daily loss, leverage, heat, and position count are first-class UI; rejected trades return the explicit gate that fired.
2. **Regime is named, not implied.** Every signal carries a regime label (Trending / Mean-Reverting / Volatile / Quiet) and a confidence score, paired with the gate result. The buyer decides whether the signal fits their framework.
3. **The buyer's capital stays in the buyer's account.** No custody, no pooled capital, no fund vehicle. Exchange is the source of truth. Custody-free is structural, not negotiable.
4. **Anti-overclaim is built in.** No "production-ready" until our published Production Candidate Criteria pass. No "guaranteed" anywhere. No leaderboards. No testimonials presented as endorsement.
5. **UAE-built, MENA-rooted, custody-free by structural choice.** Founder-resident in UAE, target geography UAE/MENA + global EN, US blocked at signup until licensure is decided. Geographic posture is a feature, not a constraint.

These are **proof-style** distinctions: each can be observed in 30 seconds of using the product, or in 2 minutes of reading the website. They do not require the buyer to take our word.

## 7. What it should not claim (locked guardrails)

A consolidated list of phrases and framings that are disallowed across all surfaces — website, sales, onboarding, content, social. Each is locked.

| Disallowed | Why disallowed | Allowed alternative |
|---|---|---|
| `production-ready` | Until PCC v2 §8 passes | `validation-phase`, `validated on testnet`, `30-day cohort pending` |
| `guaranteed` (any form) | Anti-overclaim | `gate-enforced`, `rule-respected`, `evidence-led` |
| `10x your account`, `boost`, `crush the market` | Anti-overclaim, brand voice | `enforce your framework`, `survive volatility`, `respect your rules` |
| `autonomous trading` | Custody-free, user-authorized posture | `gated trade preparation`, `signal evaluation` |
| `we trade for you` | Custody-free, user-authorized posture | `you authorize each trade against your gates` |
| `our signals` (as a deliverable) | Anti-signal-service positioning | `our scanner surfaces setups; your gates decide` |
| `institutional-grade` (without evidence) | Reserved phrase | Use only where Desk Full v2 surface and documented criteria support it |
| `best-in-class`, `market-leading`, `world's first` | Anti-overclaim, unverifiable | Stop. Restate the specific feature. |
| `live performance`, `track record` | Until §8 passes + counsel review | `cohort observation`, `testnet-validated` |
| `risk-free` (any context) | Anti-overclaim | `risk-gated`, `rule-enforced` |
| Customer testimonials presented as endorsement of returns | Anti-overclaim | Quote about *process*, not *performance*; with attribution |
| Fund-formation framing for Solo PM marketing | Counsel-line; locked v1 §3.0 | Discipline + multi-account + audit-grade phrasing |
| Native US user copy / unblocked US signup | Regulatory posture | "Available in UAE / MENA / global EN. US: not yet supported." |
