## 1. Folder purpose

This folder defines **how CoinScopeAI goes to market** in a way that is credible, staged, and aligned with current product readiness. It is deliberately conservative — emphasizing trust-heavy acquisition over hype — because the product's commercial credibility is structurally fragile during validation phase.

The folder answers four operator questions:

1. **What's the GTM motion?** — `gtm-strategy.md`
2. **Where do we put founder time and budget?** — `channel-prioritization.md`
3. **What does "launch" mean and how do we sequence it?** — `launch-plan.md`
4. **How do we avoid looking like a black-box trading promise?** — `trust-first-growth.md`

It does not invent a separate GTM thesis. It operationalizes the locked one (`07-gtm-strategy.md` + `_phase-2/_gtm/01-go-to-market-strategy-v1.md`) into implementation-grade guidance for a founder running this against the P1 (Jun–Jul 2026) cohort cap and the P2 (Aug–Sep 2026) public launch.

## 3. Why this folder matters

GTM is the surface where positioning meets the buyer. CoinScopeAI's positioning is **anti-overclaim, capital-preservation-first, validation-phase honest**. A typical crypto-product GTM motion (paid ads + influencers + leaderboards + affiliate funnels) is structurally incompatible with that positioning. Running one would burn the moat we are trying to build.

The corollary: **lower-volume, slower, more disciplined acquisition is not a constraint — it is the strategy.** The 40-user P1 cohort cap is deliberate. Founder-led distribution through methodology channels is deliberate. Deferring paid acquisition until Trader CAC validates (M5+) is deliberate.

This folder makes that deliberateness operational so that founder time, content output, and partnership decisions are all aligned to the same strategy — and so that an opportunistic offer to "scale faster with a $5k/mo Twitter influencer deal" can be declined with a documented reason rather than a gut call.
