## 1. Channel philosophy

Channels are evaluated on three dimensions, in order:

1. **Cohort-quality signal:** Does this channel reach P1 Omar (and selectively P3 Layla, P2 Karim) in a way that pre-qualifies them by methodology, account size, and geography? Or does it pull anti-ICP traffic?
2. **Trust congruence:** Does the channel's medium and tone match CoinScopeAI's anti-overclaim, methodology-first posture? Or does it require us to bend the voice to perform on the channel?
3. **Founder-time efficiency:** Does the channel produce qualified candidates per hour of founder time at a defensible rate? At validation phase, founder time is the binding constraint.

A channel that wins on (3) but loses on (1) or (2) is rejected. Volume without cohort quality is a liability, not an asset.

## 3. Channel ranking with rationale

### Top 5 prioritized — ranking

| Rank | Channel | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | **C5 Curated cohort recruitment** | The cohort is the marketing. The 40 users determine P2's viability. Highest ROI on founder time during the P1 window. Anything that competes for founder time during this window competes against this. |
| **2** | **C1 Founder Substack — methodology longform** | Builds the durable, asynchronous demand surface. Each post compounds. P1 Omar evaluates over weeks; the Substack is the asset they read during evaluation. Anchors the methodology-disclosure-as-demand-surface mechanism. |
| **3** | **C4 `/methodology` + `/what-we-dont-do` pages** | Build-once / refresh-quarterly. Lowest ongoing cost per qualified visitor. Highest trust-congruence — the page itself signals what the product is about. Required underpinning for C1 and C2. |
| **4** | **C3 Closed methodology Discords** | Highest cohort-quality per founder hour; bounded by access (only ~3–5 groups exist that are ICP-aligned and welcoming of founder participation). Cannot scale linearly, but the cohort it produces is unmatched. |
| **5** | **C2 Applied-quant Twitter / X** | Lower per-unit conversion than the others, but high incidental signal — surfaces P1 candidates we wouldn't otherwise find, and amplifies Substack content. Voice-discipline-sensitive — easy to drift. |

### Why this ranking

The first three channels are **earned-asset channels** — they build over time and cost only founder attention. The fourth is **bounded-access** — the access itself is the asset. The fifth is **interactive-amplification** — it multiplies the reach of the others.

There is no rank-6 in P1. Adding a sixth channel during P1 dilutes the founder time required to execute the top five well.

## 4. Best channels for trust-heavy, niche trader audiences

The buyer pattern (per `04-icp-and-segmentation/primary-icp.md` §5):

> "Slow, reading-heavy, skeptical-by-default. Discovery via peer recommendation in closed community, or long-form content via Substack / Twitter. First-look reads the website thoroughly; checks for performance promises (red flag). Free-tier evaluation lasts days–weeks. Trial / paid evaluation runs in parallel with manual workflow for 2–6 weeks. Conversion is earned, not closed."

This pattern matches **only** asynchronous, methodology-rich, founder-mediated channels:

| Buyer behavior | Channel implication |
|---|---|
| Reads long-form before purchase | Substack, longform Twitter threads, methodology pages |
| Checks for performance promises (red flag if found) | `/what-we-dont-do` + anti-overclaim copy on every surface |
| Slow evaluation (days–weeks on Free, 2–6 weeks paid) | No urgency-driven funnels; no countdown timers; no flash promos |
| Peer-recommendation-driven | Closed methodology Discords; founder presence in those communities |
| Skeptical-by-default | Founder named, contactable; named author bylines; published incident postmortems |

The implication: **what looks like high-friction acquisition is actually filter-aligned acquisition.** The channels that work are the ones that match the buyer's evaluation pattern. Trying to compress the evaluation cycle (urgency timers, "limited spots") triggers the buyer's skepticism response and destroys the candidacy.

## 5. Low-cost / high-signal channels

Channels that produce qualified candidates per hour of founder time at the highest defensible rate during P1.

| Channel | Cost (USD) | Founder hours / week | Signal | Confidence band |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **C5 Curated cohort recruitment** | $0 | 4–8 hours, P1 ramp | Highest — direct candidate qualification | High |
| **C3 Closed Discord participation** | $0 | 2–4 hours, ongoing | Very high — peer-context candidates | High |
| **C4 `/methodology` + `/what-we-dont-do` pages** | $0 incremental (pages exist) | <1 hour / week refresh | High — passive qualifier | High |
| **C1 Substack longform** | ~$50/year hosting | 4–6 hours per post | High — compounding asset | High |
| **C2 Twitter / X engagement** | $0 | 2–4 hours / week | Medium — supplements C1 reach | Medium |

**Total weekly founder hours during P1 across these five channels: 12–22 hours.** The remainder of founder time goes to product, support, and operations per `01-executive-summary/strategic-priorities.md`.

The implication: **low-cost / high-signal channels are deliberately constrained to what one disciplined founder can sustain at quality.** Any channel that demands more time than this displaces a higher-priority operational task.

## 6. High-risk channels to defer

| Channel | Risk profile | Defer until |
|---|---|---|
| **Paid acquisition (any platform)** | Burns capital before CAC validates; ROI cannot be measured against an unstable cohort baseline | M5+, only if PP7 holds |
| **Influencer marketing on crypto channels** | Brand contamination; voice drift under contractual obligation; anti-ICP audience adjacency | Indefinite — anti-channel |
| **Press blitz at launch** | Audience mismatch surge; support breaks; cohort signal degrades | One placement at P2; no broader push |
| **Conference / meetup paid sponsorships** | Capacity expensive; trust-positive but bounded ROI | P3+, invitation-only |
| **Affiliate program with payouts** | Anti-overclaim risk under affiliate-content discretion | Indefinite — revisit post-P5 only |
| **YouTube channel** | High production cost; audience-mismatched; anti-overclaim risk in video format under engagement pressure | Possibly post-P5 if a specific creator joins as a documented contractor under brand voice |
| **Discord community owned by CoinScopeAI** | Support load multiplier; voice-incongruence risk; cohort-quality dilution if open-access | Cohort-only Discord at P3+; never open-access at P1–P2 |

The defer-not-cancel rule: **a deferred channel keeps a documented re-evaluation date**, not a "maybe someday" status. Paid acquisition's earliest revisit is M5+ post-CAC. Conferences are P3+ on invitation. Each has a trigger.

## 7. Content-led vs. community-led vs. direct outreach trade-offs

Three motion shapes, each with different cost and signal profiles. CoinScopeAI uses all three, weighted toward content-led during P1.

| Motion | Cost shape | Signal shape | Best for | Worst for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Content-led** (Substack, Twitter, methodology pages) | Front-loaded (writing time); compounds | Asynchronous; signal lags creation by weeks | Building durable demand surface; reaching slow-evaluating P1 Omar | Time-sensitive recruitment (cohort opens in 2 weeks) |
| **Community-led** (closed Discords, peer networks, founder personal network) | Continuous (ongoing presence); bounded by access | High-signal, low-volume | Cohort-quality candidates; named introductions | Volume scaling (caps at access ceiling) |
| **Direct outreach** (curated cohort recruitment) | Concentrated burst (P1 ramp) | High-signal, high-control | Filling specific cohort slots with specific candidate profiles | Sustained acquisition (founder bandwidth caps it) |

### Recommended weighting during P1

| Motion | P1 weighting | P2 weighting | P3+ weighting |
|---|---|---|---|
| Content-led | **50%** of GTM time | 50% | 60% (compounds) |
| Community-led | 25% | 25% | 25% |
| Direct outreach | 25% (cohort filling) | 15% (Desk Preview specifically) | 15% (Desk Full v2 candidates pre-P5) |

The content-led majority is deliberate: **it is the only motion that scales with the brand rather than with founder time.** Community-led and direct outreach do not scale linearly past founder bandwidth; content does.

## 8. Partnership-led channel possibilities

Partnerships are **deferred until P3 close at earliest**, after cohort signal accumulates (PP4, PP6, PP8). Three candidate categories, with constraints.

### 8.1 Methodology / education partnerships

Partnership candidates:

- Position-sizing methodology authors with newsletters/courses that reach P1 Omar
- Quant-trading educators with cohort-based programs
- Trading-journal vendors (Tradervue, Edgewonk) — adjacent rather than competitive

Mechanism: cross-promotion of methodology content; bundled discount for partner's audience (≤25%, time-bounded, no stacking — per `07-packaging-and-pricing/trial-and-discount-policy.md` §6).

Constraint: each partnership must pass brand-voice review. No partner promotes CoinScopeAI as "alpha-generating" or "production-ready" — the partner's copy is reviewed before activation.

### 8.2 Prop-firm partnerships

Partnership candidates:

- Funded-trader programs (FTMO, Apex, Topstep) — secondary persona deferred per `04-icp-and-segmentation/secondary-icps.md`

Mechanism: structured discount for funded traders who pass our anti-ICP filter; or co-marketing aimed at the post-funded "I have a real account now" segment.

Constraint: prop-firm partnerships specifically require careful brand-voice alignment. Many prop firms market with high-leverage and high-conversion language that conflicts with capital-preservation positioning. **DECISION NEEDED — pre-partnership brand-voice screening protocol.**

### 8.3 Vendor partnerships

Partnership candidates:

- Adjacent vendors in our supply chain (e.g., CoinGlass, Tradefeeds, Claude API) — mostly *customer* relationships rather than *partnership* relationships, but worth assessing for co-content opportunities

Mechanism: technical methodology content co-authored with vendor's domain experts; mutual reference in case studies (post-cohort signal).

Constraint: vendor partnerships must not signal endorsement of CoinScopeAI's product readiness. Co-content is methodology only.

### What is **not** considered a partnership

- Affiliate / referral programs — locked off at P1–P2.
- Influencer arrangements — anti-channel.
- White-label or OEM deals — not on roadmap.
- Sponsored content — anti-channel.
