## 1. GTM philosophy for CoinScopeAI

Five operating beliefs, in order of weight. Each is a binding constraint on every GTM decision.

1. **Trust compounds; hype evaporates.** A disciplined buyer who trusts the product references it once a year for a decade. A hyped sign-up churns inside 60 days and tells the cohort. CoinScopeAI's positioning forces a slow, trust-heavy motion — and the GTM strategy must be congruent with that, not in tension with it.
2. **The cohort is the marketing.** The 40 P1 users are not "early customers." They are the case study that earns the next 400 users in P2. If the cohort is mismanaged for the sake of growth, the next 400 do not arrive.
3. **Distribution must look like the product.** The product enforces discipline; the distribution motion must be disciplined. Buyer-side reading-heavy, founder-led, methodology-first — it matches what the buyer is. A scrappy meme-driven funnel against a "capital preservation, by default" product would invalidate the positioning.
4. **Constraints reveal the strategy.** US blocked at signup, sub-$5k routed to "we'll be back", 40-user P1 cap, no paid acquisition before CAC validates — these are not handicaps; they are the strategy. A wider funnel today is a smaller cohort signal tomorrow.
5. **Anti-overclaim is the moat, not the marketing.** The mechanism that protects the brand also constrains the GTM. Every channel, every claim, every announcement passes anti-overclaim review before shipping. Acquired trust is worth more than acquired users.

The synthesis: **CoinScopeAI's GTM is a slow, founder-led, methodology-first, trust-compounding motion that filters cohort quality at every step and refuses to scale acquisition until the validation evidence justifies it.**

## 2. Recommended initial GTM motion

**The Narrow Ship motion**, locked v1 in `_phase-2/_gtm/01-go-to-market-strategy-v1.md` and restated here for operator clarity.

### 2.1 What Narrow Ship looks like, week-by-week

| Window | Who's reachable | What we do | What we do not do |
|---|---|---|---|

| **P1 soft launch** (Jun–Jul 2026) | 40 founder-cohort users | Curated cohort onboarding; weekly cohort observation; product-tier support; methodology cadence sustained | No public launch announcements; no acquisition hooks beyond cohort cap; no affiliate program |
| **P1 → P2 transition** (late Jul → early Aug 2026) | P1 cohort feedback closing the loop | Cohort interviews; persona reconfirmation; vendor runbook dry-run; Desk Preview at quality bar | No public launch until P1 exit gate clears (per §14.1) |
| **P2 public launch** (Aug–Sep 2026) | Broader P1 Omar + early P3 Layla; P2 Karim watch-list | Single launch announcement (`launch-plan.md` §4); 60-day founder-cohort window opens; one ≤25%/≤30-day promo allowed | No paid acquisition (still gated until CAC validates at M5+); no leaderboards; no press blitz |
| **P3 stabilization** (Oct–Dec 2026) | Trader at scale; Desk Preview filling | CAC validation work (`trust-first-growth.md` §7); content depth deepens; partnership candidacy assessed | No paid acquisition until BMA5 (`business-model-summary.md` §8) is checked |

### 2.2 The four mechanisms inside Narrow Ship

The motion runs on four mechanisms working together:

**A. Founder-led distribution.** Founder is the named author of methodology content, the named contact for cohort onboarding, and the named voice on Telegram/Twitter for product communication. Trust is earned through visibility of a real human, not a brand mascot.

**B. Methodology-disclosure-as-demand-surface.** The product's analytical engine is publicly documented (`coinscope.ai/methodology`). Detailed posts on regime classification logic, risk-gate math, and position-sizing transparency double as both education and product credibility. Visitors who read the methodology and stay are pre-qualified.

**C. Curated cohort recruitment.** P1's 40 users are recruited through a named-introduction or qualified-application process (decision pending — see `launch-plan.md` §3). They are not first-come-first-served. The cohort is the experiment; experiments are designed.

**D. Anti-overclaim copy discipline.** Every external surface — pricing page, about page, launch announcement, Telegram alert, Substack post — passes brand-voice review (`business-plan/09-brand-messaging.md`). The same machinery that protects the brand internally constrains acquisition externally.

These four mechanisms are not optional combinations. Removing any one degrades the others. The motion is a system, not a buffet.

## 4. Should founder-led GTM dominate early?

**Yes — exclusively in P1 and primarily through P2.** Locked.

### Why founder-led dominance is the right choice for CoinScopeAI specifically

- **Trust posture is structurally founder-mediated.** "UAE-built, named-founder, custody-free by structural choice" cannot be delegated to an outsourced growth agency without breaking the message.
- **Cohort cap matches founder bandwidth.** 40 users in P1 is at the upper bound of what a founder + light contractor support can onboard with care. A larger funnel breaks support, breaks anti-overclaim discipline under pressure, and breaks cohort observation.
- **Anti-overclaim discipline lives in the founder's voice.** Brand-voice rules are enforceable when the founder is the named author. They become a liability when distributed to growth contractors who optimize for conversion rather than positioning.
- **The buyer is suspicious of agency-driven growth.** P1 Omar specifically reads "growth-hacked" and "viral-loop" patterns as red flags. Founder-led methodology content is the inverse signal.
- **Cost-efficiency is necessary at this stage.** No paid acquisition + founder-led means CAC ≈ 0 + founder time. The cost discipline this enforces is a feature for the financial model.

### What founder-led GTM means concretely in P1

- Founder is the named author of every Substack post, every Twitter thread, every cohort-recruitment outreach, every onboarding kickoff message.
- Founder attends each onboarding kickoff for cohort users (P3 Layla candidates and edge-case P1 Omars).
- Founder runs the P1 cohort observation cadence (weekly check-ins, structured feedback capture, refund-reason capture).
- Founder is the named voice on `@ScoopyAI_bot` Telegram for product-tier communication and incident comms.
- Founder is the named author of the P2 public-launch announcement.

### When founder-led begins to scale (not before)

- **P3 (Oct–Dec 2026):** A second voice may be added for content cadence — vetted contractor, brand-voice-aligned, named on bylines. Founder remains the primary voice.
- **Post-P5 (mid-2027+):** Channel-mix lock evolves; community-led and partnership-led mechanisms layer on. Paid acquisition only if Trader CAC validated and BMA5 holds.

The escalation rule: **founder-led time only frees up after the validation evidence justifies hiring growth into the gap.** Inverting the order is the most common GTM mistake at this stage.

## 5. How the product should be introduced while real-capital use remains gated

The validation-phase posture is **not a marketing problem to solve around** — it is a marketing asset to be honest about. Three principles:

### 5.1 Lead with the gate, not despite it

The pricing page, the about page, the launch announcement, every product surface includes:

> *"Testnet only. 30-day validation phase. No real capital. v0.[X]."*

This is a brand voice rule (per Scoopy custom instructions) and a credibility claim. Buyers who reject the framing self-select out (anti-ICP filter). Buyers who accept it self-select in (ICP confirm).

### 5.2 Reframe the gate as a quality signal, not a limitation

Acceptable copy:

- *"We will not market the engine as production-ready until our published Production Candidate Criteria pass."*
- *"Capital preservation is the default. Real-capital deployment is gated by criteria, not by our willingness to ship."*
- *"You can run the engine on Binance Testnet today. Real-capital deployment opens through a phased ramp once §8 Capital Cap evidence accumulates."*

Unacceptable copy (anti-overclaim violations):

- ~~*"Going live soon!"*~~ — signals impending unsupported claims
- ~~*"Beta = production once you trust it"*~~ — undermines the gate's meaning
- ~~*"Testnet results indicate real-capital potential of X%"*~~ — performance promise from non-production data

### 5.3 Make the gate path visible

The product publishes:

- The published Production Candidate Criteria v2 — `coinscope.ai/pcc-v2` (or equivalent).
- The §8 Capital Cap and phased-ramp framework, in user-readable summary.
- A `Validation_Phase_Exit_Memo` template, made public when filed.
- The decision log entry for any phase advance.

Visibility is the differentiator. Most competitors hide their readiness state. CoinScopeAI publishes it. That is itself the positioning.

## 6. Proof points needed before scaling acquisition

Scaling acquisition = expanding beyond founder-led + adding paid channels. This requires **earned evidence**, not aspirational claims. Eight proof points, in dependency order.

| # | Proof point | What it proves | Evidence source | Required for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **PP1** | PCC v2 G1–G4 + §8 pass | Engine and risk gates meet documented criteria | `Validation_Phase_Exit_Memo` filed | All acquisition scaling |
| **PP2** | 40-user P1 cohort onboarded without stop-the-line | Operational discipline holds at the cohort cap | §13.4 KPI feed; zero §14 stop-the-line triggers | P2 public launch |
| **PP3** | Persona reconfirmation from §3.7 interviews | Personas P1/P2/P3 represent the actual paying cohort | ≥12 interviews completed; persona file updated | P2 public launch + content motion sustained |
| **PP4** | P1 cohort 30-day retention ≥ assumed threshold | Cohort is paying for value, not extracting | `business-plan/13-kpi-okr.md` MAVT north-star | P2 expansion of funnel |
| **PP5** | Vendor failure-mode runbook dry-run executed | Ops can absorb a vendor incident at cohort scale | `Vendor_Failure_Mode_Mapping_v1` exercised | P2 public launch |
| **PP6** | Desk Preview at quality bar by P1 close | Tier ladder is coherent end-to-end | Per `06-product-strategy/feature-prioritization.md` | P2 Desk Preview funnel optimization |
| **PP7** | Trader CAC under M5 LTV/CAC ≥ 3:1 floor | Acquisition can become economically efficient | §6.9 sensitivity model + cohort-source attribution | Paid acquisition unblock (D1 trigger) |
| **PP8** | One incident handled cleanly with public postmortem | Operational transparency holds under pressure | First incident report published | Public-trust acceleration; partnership candidacy |

**The rule:** **Acquisition scales after evidence accumulates, not before.** Inverting this — scaling acquisition to "manufacture" evidence — is the most common existential mistake at this stage.

## 8. GTM risks to avoid

Twelve failure modes that have killed trust-sensitive trading products historically. Each maps to a specific guard.

| # | Risk | Why it kills CoinScopeAI specifically | Guard |
|---|---|---|---|
| **GR1** | **Premature paid acquisition** | Spends capital before CAC validates; wastes founder time on funnel optimization that hasn't earned the right | D1 — paid acquisition gated to M5+ + PP7 |
| **GR2** | **Influencer marketing in crypto Twitter** | Brand contamination by adjacent anti-ICP audience; cohort-quality dilution | Anti-channel — never |
| **GR3** | **Leaderboards / public benchmarks** (testnet data) | Anti-overclaim violation; performance promise from non-production data | D12 — never until §8 + counsel |
| **GR4** | **Press blitz at P1 launch** | Spike of mismatched audience; support breaks; cohort signal degrades | One placement at P2; never at P1 |
| **GR5** | **Affiliate revenue share** | Misaligned incentives; brand-voice drift; anti-ICP audience adjacency | Never at P1–P2; revisit post-P5 |
| **GR6** | **"Production-ready" claim before §8 passes** | Existential — kills the moat in one tweet | Brand-voice rule; review pass before any external claim |
| **GR7** | **Cross-promotion with signal groups, copy-trade, leverage maximizers** | Brand contamination; cohort signal pollution | §5.3.3 — locked anti-channel |
| **GR8** | **Mass-market crypto-Reddit / TikTok presence** | Audience mismatch; anti-ICP attraction | Never at P1–P2; never as a primary channel |
| **GR9** | **Discount theatre** (sales, flash-promos, urgency timers) | Erodes price credibility; signals desperation | One time-bounded P2 promo; nothing else |
| **GR10** | **Manufactured testimonials / endorsements** | Anti-overclaim violation; trust collapse on discovery | Locked — no testimonials presented as endorsement |
| **GR11** | **Feature-complete launch announcement before Desk Preview is at quality bar** | Promise > delivery; anti-overclaim violation | P2 launch announcement only after PP6 |
| **GR12** | **Scaling support promises before ops capacity exists** | Trader SLA stated as guarantee; founder-led ops can't sustain; trust damage | Trader SLA is best-effort; documented in `13-support-and-trust-ops/` |

The combined rule: **a GTM action that would feel normal at a typical SaaS company is often anti-fit at CoinScopeAI.** When in doubt, the test is: *"Does this action increase cohort-signal quality, or just signup volume?"* Volume-only actions are deferred or rejected.
