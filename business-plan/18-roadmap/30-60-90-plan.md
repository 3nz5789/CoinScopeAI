# 30-60-90 Plan

## Anchor date and assumptions

- **Today:** 2026-05-08
- **Phase:** P0 (validation cohort, cap 40, Binance Testnet only)
- **NSM:** VCE (Validated Cohort Engagement) — transitions to TRAS at P1 narrow ship
- **Real-capital authorization:** NO (default; non-delegable)
- **PCC v2 state:** REQUIRED INPUT — assumed mid-progression (G1–G2 in place, G3 in flight). The plan below is conservative about G3.

If actual PCC state is materially different from assumed, re-baseline the plan in the next weekly review and log the re-baseline in `21-decision-log`.

## Categorization

Each item below is tagged:

- **D** = Discovery (figure out what to build / how)
- **E** = Execution (build / ship / deploy)
- **H** = Hardening (test / validate / instrument / runbook)

A roadmap that is 100% execution is brittle. A healthy 30-day window is roughly 20% discovery, 50% execution, 30% hardening at our stage.

## Discovery vs execution vs hardening — by window

| Window | D | E | H | Total | Mix |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 30 days | 4 | 5 | 5 | 14 | 29 / 36 / 36% — hardening-heavy, correct for P0 close-out |
| 60 days | 3 | 5 | 4 | 12 | 25 / 42 / 33% — execution rises as P1 ships |
| 90 days | 3 | 2 | 7 | 12 | 25 / 17 / 58% — hardening-heavy as P1 stabilizes |

The mix shape is intentional. **Hardening-heavy windows precede every phase transition.** A roadmap that flattens into mostly execution is a roadmap that stops validating its own assumptions.

## What forces re-baselining the plan (mid-flight)

- PCC v2 backward gate movement — freeze plan, re-baseline.
- P1 incident with paid-customer impact — pause monetization decisions, re-baseline.
- Vendor disruption (CoinGlass change, Binance enforcement event) — re-baseline.
- Founder unavailability >5 business days — pause execution items, hold hardening items.
- Refund rate spike >5% MRR in any month — pause growth items, focus on root cause.

If any of those occur, re-baseline at the next weekly review and log it.
