## Why POSITIONING matters specifically for CoinScopeAI

Positioning is the *load-bearing claim* of the business. For a crypto-AI trading product in 2026, the positioning sentence does three jobs at once:

1. **Sets the trust contract.** Every adjective in the sentence is something the buyer will hold us to. "Institutional-grade" is a contract; "AI-driven" is a contract; "for funds" is a contract.
2. **Filters traffic.** A correctly tight position drives wrong-fit traffic away. That is *the goal* — we want fewer, better-qualified leads in P0 and P1.
3. **Pre-empts comparison.** If we don't claim a position, the buyer slots us into the closest loud competitor's category. That's worse than any tight claim we could make.

For CSAI specifically, the position must reconcile three forces:

- **Engine reality** — testnet-first, real-capital-gated, drawdown-disciplined.
- **Brand voice** — anti-overclaim, evidence-led, methodical.
- **Conversion need** — the position must still make the buyer click *something*.

The Scoopy operating principles already encode this. Phase 1 just translates them into one external sentence.

## Anti-claim list (locked in Phase 1)

These are claims CoinScopeAI **will not make** in P1 marketing, product copy, or any external surface:

1. **No P&L claims.** No "X% returns", no "Y% win rate", no historical performance numbers without the full validation methodology, sample size, and time window — and never on hero / ad surfaces.
2. **No automation promise.** We do not say "trades for you", "auto-trade", "set and forget", or any phrase that implies delegated capital-at-risk decisions. We *can* say "executes on rules you've gated".
3. **No "institutional-grade" until earned.** Phrase is forbidden in P1 copy. Earliest it can appear: after Desk-Preview cohort closes with documented evidence.
4. **No "production-ready", "battle-tested", "proven", or "live-trading"** until PCC v2 G1–G4 are all green and §8 Capital Cap Phase 1 is open. Until then, copy must use *validated on testnet*, *shadow-tested*, *30-day cohort pending*.

These four are non-negotiable in Phase 1. Adding more anti-claims is welcome; loosening them requires a Decision Log entry.

## Failure modes to avoid

- **Multi-claim soup.** Trying to position as risk-first AND AI AND institutional AND for-funds AND retail-friendly. Pick one front-door.
- **Positioning by aspiration.** Writing the sentence we want to earn, not the one we can defend. P1 copy is what is *true today*, not what is true at Desk Full v2.
- **Letting the engine's marketing-style jargon leak.** Never use "alpha", "unfair edge", or any zero-sum phrasing in product-tier surfaces.
- **Forgetting the disclosures.** The positioning sentence should always be readable next to the risk disclaimer without contradiction.
