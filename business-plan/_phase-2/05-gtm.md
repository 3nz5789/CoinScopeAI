## 1. Purpose

Lock the **Narrow Ship demand-generation system** for P1 (Jun–Jul 2026): the pricing-page surface, the founder-cohort recruiting motion, the beta-cohort conversion offer, the launch sequencing across P0 → P1 → P2 → P5, and the anti-overclaim audit standard for every public claim. Phase 2 GTM is *mechanics + posture*, not *channel-mix lock*. Channel-mix selection (founder-led vs content vs partnerships vs paid) is deferred to Phase 3 per Phase 2 charter §2 + §8.

## 2. Why this matters specifically for CoinScopeAI

- **Capital-preservation positioning makes most demand channels anti-fit by construction.** Crypto-trading Twitter, signal-group cross-promotions, leverage-maximizer influencer deals, copy-trade affiliate networks — all category-typical, all anti-ICP per §5.3.3 + `_packaging/05-packaging-friction-review.md` design rule 8. Phase 2 GTM has to pre-commit on what we *don't* do before Phase 3 picks what we do.
- **Validation phase elevates trust load on every public surface.** The pricing page, the founder's writing, every outbound message bears "Testnet only · 30-day validation phase · No real capital." The discipline that protects the product also bounds how aggressively we can market it.
- **Founder-led distribution is the realistic v1 motion.** Per `_support/01-support-operating-model.md` — founder is the operator at sole-prop scale. GTM at P1 cannot assume a marketing team; it must run on founder time + asynchronous content + the artifacts already built (methodology docs, pricing page, status page).
- **Founder-cohort window is a one-shot 60-day GTM event.** Per `_pricing/03-monthly-vs-annual-offer-structure.md` §6 — the 60-day window starts at public launch. GTM Phase 2 designs how that window is recruited, communicated, and closed without drift to "lifetime" framing.
- **Pricing-page spec is in PACKAGING; copy + voice + register are in BRAND; CTA → conversion is here.** GTM doesn't redesign the pricing page; GTM owns the *flow* into it and the *conversion mechanics* on it.
- **Real-capital language traces to PCC v2 §8, every time.** GTM copy has the same anti-overclaim discipline as product + support. "Trade live capital" / "production-ready" / "battle-tested" — all PCC §8 violations until §8 gates pass.
- **No paid acquisition at v1.** Per Phase 2 charter §2 — paid acquisition is Phase 3 explicitly. GTM at v1 is organic + founder-led; the spec must reflect that, not pretend a media budget exists.
- **Phase 5 Desk Full v2 launch (Mar–May 2027) needs sequencing now.** Even though it's >10 months out, the GTM sequencing framework (`04`) must reserve runway for it so we don't accidentally over-invest the P1/P2 motion into a channel mix that won't carry DF v2 buyers.

3. **ASSUMPTION** — Validation-phase disclaimer + PCC v2 §8 framing is *positioning advantage* with the disciplined-trader ICP, not a conversion drag. Validate via cohort feedback on conversion intent.

## 7. Failure modes to avoid

- **Channel-mix lock under Phase 2 cover.** Phase 2 GTM must NOT lock channel mix; that's Phase 3. Avoid commitments that pre-determine Phase 3 ("we'll be on Twitter" / "we'll do paid Google" / "we'll partner with X"). Phase 2 commits to *posture* and *what we don't do*, not *the channel mix*.
- **Paid acquisition pre-trigger.** "Just a small test budget" violates Phase 2 charter §2 + §8 (paid is Phase 3). The test budget at P1 produces noisy data, blows founder time on attribution, and pre-anchors Phase 3 channel-mix decision. Hold the line.
- **Co-marketing with anti-ICP.** Signal groups, copy-trade products, leverage-maximizer content, "9-figure trader" influencers — endorsement-by-association violates §5.3.3 + `_packaging/05` design rule 8. Anti-channel list (G-5) makes this explicit.
- **Founder-cohort comms drift to "lifetime."** §6.10 Flag 1 violation. Canonical phrasing only: "Founder-cohort pricing — locked through your first renewal cycle, then standard pricing applies." Per `_pricing/03` §6.
- **Real-capital claims in marketing copy.** "Production-ready" / "battle-tested" / "trade live now" — all PCC v2 §8 violations until §8 gates pass. Marketing copy inherits the same anti-overclaim discipline as product + support. Per `_support/05` §7 Canonical 1.
- **Tagline drift into product surfaces.** "Trade Smarter With AI" is allowed in marketing-tier surfaces (pricing page hero, social media, advertising); the product-tier register (dashboard, signal cards, gate decisions, support replies) stays declarative + technical. Per Scoopy custom instructions.
- **"Most popular" / urgency mechanics on pricing page.** Per `_packaging/05` design rule 4 + `_pricing/02` Principle 5. Hard out.
- **Influencer / affiliate program at v1.** Per `_pricing/02` Principle 5 + `_packaging/05` Class F. Pays for adverse selection. Re-evaluate post-Phase 3 only with persona-fit-screen gating.
- **Conversion-rate vanity metrics.** Tracking signup volume without persona-fit segmentation hides whether the funnel is recruiting Omar / Karim / Layla or recruiting casual-retail. KPI dashboard must segment by persona-fit signal.
- **Demo-day theatrics.** Public timed demos / "live trading" demos / countdown launches violate the methodical BRAND voice + the validation-phase posture. The product is in a 30-day validation; the launch is methodical, not theatrical.
- **Press cycle as primary launch channel.** Press is high-variance; for a capital-preservation positioning, a single hot-take headline ("AI crypto trading bot launches") miscategorizes the product. Press is downstream of methodology + cohort, not upstream.
- **Discord / Telegram public group.** Open community without support coverage produces uncovered trust-load surfaces (per `_support/01` §3). Defer to Phase 3+ concept (`_support/04-support.md` LATER).
- **International expansion premature.** UAE/MENA + global EN is the v1 audience (per memory `project_jurisdictional`). Adding Arabic / Spanish / Mandarin localizations pre-validation drains founder time + creates surfaces that drift from canonical phrasings.
- **Pricing-page A/B tests at v1.** Phase 2 PACKAGING + PRICING locks the surface; A/B tests fragment the canonical state and produce uninterpretable cohort data. Hold pricing page stable through P0 cohort exit memo.
