## 1. The strategy in one paragraph

CoinScopeAI sells a capital-preservation-first AI-driven crypto futures system to disciplined-trader and small-fund-PM personas. P1 Narrow Ship (Jun–Jul 2026) recruits the first 100–200 paying customers organically through founder-led distribution + methodology-disclosure-as-demand-surface, with no paid acquisition, no community, no influencer / affiliate / referral programs, and no co-marketing with anti-ICP products. The 60-day founder-cohort window (post-public-launch) is the one-shot GTM event of Phase 2. Channel-mix lock (founder-led / content / partnerships / paid) is deferred to Phase 3 with explicit anti-channels listed in the Initial Channel Strategy. Phase 5 (Mar–May 2027) reserves capacity for the Desk Full v2 launch — a separate motion targeting Layla Solo PM scaling, whose recruiting starts now via the methodology + roadmap surfaces.

## 5. The "what we explicitly don't do" register

Per §5.3.3 packaging principles + `_pricing/02` Principle 5 + `_packaging/05-packaging-friction-review.md` Class F. P1 Narrow Ship explicitly does not:

1. **Run paid acquisition.** Per Phase 2 charter §2 + §8 — paid is Phase 3.
2. **Pay influencers in free tiers or affiliate kickbacks.** Pays for adverse selection.
3. **Co-market with signal groups, copy-trade products, leverage maximizers, "9-figure trader" influencers.** Endorsement-by-association violates §5.3.3.
4. **Run a referral / affiliate program.** Re-evaluate post-Phase 3 only with persona-fit-screen gating.
5. **Run "Most popular" badges, urgency timers, countdown launches, strikethrough discount theatre.** Per `_packaging/05` design rule 4.
6. **Use "lifetime / forever / always / locked-in" framing for founder-cohort.** §6.10 Flag 1 violation.
7. **Promise real-capital readiness.** "Production-ready / battle-tested / trade live now" all violate PCC v2 §8 until §8 gates pass.
8. **Run public timed demos / live-trading demos.** Theatrical, anti-methodical.
9. **Open public Discord / Telegram community at v1.** Uncovered trust-load surface (per `_support/01` §3).
10. **Press cycle as primary launch channel.** Press is downstream of methodology + cohort.
11. **A/B test pricing page.** Hold pricing stable through P0 cohort exit memo.
12. **Translate the product into other languages at v1.** UAE/MENA + global EN at v1; localization is Phase 5+ concept.

This register is durable through Phase 5 unless explicitly amended by founder + decision-log entry.

## 6. Posture commitments at v1

The GTM motion at v1 commits to these postures (G-1 option a recommended):

### Founder-led + organic + content-light

- Founder is the operator at sole-prop scale. GTM time budget caps at 5–10 hrs/week (alongside support per `_support/01` §6 + product work).
- Content cadence: weekly long-form per G-3 option (a) — methodology / case-study / process notes; not daily short-form.
- Distribution surfaces: founder's writing channel (TBD per channel strategy `02`); methodology docs; status page; pricing-page narrative; founder-direct-conversation for inbound DP / DF prospects.

### Trust posture is the differentiator

In a category that runs on signal hype + leverage glamour, the differentiator is what we don't claim. The Phase 1 anti-claim list (per `_phase-1/03-positioning.md`) is the GTM contract; every public surface is audited against it before publication.

### Methodology-as-demand-surface (G-6 option a recommended)

For Karim (P2) and Layla (P3), the methodology page is the primary demand surface — they read it before they evaluate the product. Treating it as primary demand surface (not just trust insurance) means:

- Methodology page is well-trafficked (links from every public surface footer).
- Methodology page → signup conversion is tracked as a KPI (per ONBOARDING `_onboarding/02` cohort segmentation).
- Methodology page is updated with the engine; no stale content.
- "Read the methodology" is a CTA on its own merit, not just a credibility hedge.

For Omar (P1), methodology page is post-signup trust-confirmation rather than pre-signup demand surface — Omar reads it after seeing the demo gate decision (per `_onboarding/04` Omar overlay).

### Founder-cohort window is the one-shot GTM event

The 60-day post-public-launch founder-cohort window is the load-bearing P1 GTM event:

- Pre-launch: waitlist motion (per `02-initial-channel-strategy.md` + NEXT `Waitlist and Early Access Motion`) builds the audience that converts at window-open.
- Window-open day: launch comms (NEXT `Trust-First Launch Campaign Plan`) routes waitlist + organic traffic through pricing page.
- During window: founder-cohort copy on pricing page + onboarding (per `_onboarding/01-first-time-user-journey.md` gate 0 + gate 5).
- Window-close: 14-day-prior expiration notification (per `_pricing/03-monthly-vs-annual-offer-structure.md` §8); standard pricing applies thereafter.
- Anti-pattern: do not extend the window mid-launch. Extension reads as "we needed to bribe people" + violates time-bounded discipline.
