## 1. Channel posture taxonomy

Every channel candidate gets one of four postures:

| Posture | Definition | v1 GTM behavior |
|---|---|---|
| **IN-V1** | Channel is in scope at P1 Narrow Ship; aligns with founder-led + organic + content-light posture | Founder invests time; tracked in KPI dashboard |
| **CANDIDATE-P3** | Channel is potentially in scope at Phase 3 channel-mix lock; trust-aligned, persona-fit credible, but requires Phase 3 evaluation against KPI baseline | Documented; not invested in at v1 |
| **DEFERRED** | Channel is structurally deferred to Phase 5+ (e.g., international expansion, Phase 5 DF v2 motion) | Documented; not invested in at v1 or Phase 3 |
| **ANTI-CHANNEL** | Channel is incompatible with capital-preservation positioning + anti-overclaim discipline; explicit decline | Founder does not engage; documented for Phase 3 decision-space |

## 5. Reasoning per anti-channel category

### Why "pays for adverse selection" disqualifies a channel

A channel that compensates third-parties per signup (affiliate / referral / influencer) creates adverse selection by construction: the people referring care about the payout, not the persona-fit. For a capital-preservation product, attracting buyers who care about the discount mechanic rather than the methodology fits is a higher-cost-than-CAC signal — they'll churn faster and complain louder, and the support load + refund volume erode the savings. This is `_packaging/05-packaging-friction-review.md` Class F made operational.

### Why "anti-ICP bundling" disqualifies a channel

Co-marketing creates endorsement-by-association. Even if the co-marketed product is reasonable on its own, the act of pairing brands tells our audience "these two products belong together." For CSAI, pairing with signal groups, copy-trade products, or leverage maximizers would tell Karim and Layla that we're in the same product category as those — which we explicitly aren't. §5.3.3 + §3.5 + Canonical 4 (anti-ICP decline) define the boundary; co-marketing crosses it.

### Why "format incompatible with anti-overclaim" disqualifies a channel

Some formats (TikTok-style short-form, meme posts, daily engagement-bait) functionally require simplification + emotional language to perform. The product-tier register (technical, terse, declarative) doesn't perform in those formats. The choice is either to drift our voice (which violates BRAND posture) or to perform poorly in the format (which produces negative signal). Either way, the format extracts a brand cost.

### Why "validation-phase posture conflict" disqualifies a channel

Live streams of trading would surface the canonical 5 risk tokens + PCC v2 §8 on every screen-share, but the format implies execution + "watch me trade" engagement. During validation phase, this is incoherent — we're saying "testnet only, no real capital" while the format implies we're trading. Defer until PCC v2 §8 gates pass; then re-evaluate as a Phase 5+ candidate.
