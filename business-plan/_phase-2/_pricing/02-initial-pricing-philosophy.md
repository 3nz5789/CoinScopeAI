## 1. The one-sentence philosophy

**Pricing earns trust by being honest, predictable, and tied to value delivered — not by being the lowest, the loudest, or the most "premium."**

Every pricing decision is audited against this sentence. Tactics that feel clever but violate honesty / predictability / value-tie are out by construction.

---

## 2. The seven durable principles

### Principle 1 — Capital preservation governs the offer, not just the engine

Pricing reflects who we want as customers. Sub-$5k disciplined traders are future-ICP, not adversarial — they get Free Scope B with "we'll be back" framing per §3.5, no upgrade pressure. Pricing posture toward casual-retail buyers who want execution maximalism: not interested. We do not price to attract anyone who would be hurt by our product.

### Principle 2 — One number, one tier, no ambiguity

Single-number pricing per tier. Monthly + annual total + founder-cohort all visible. No "starting at," no "as low as," no strikethrough anchoring, no "contact sales" gates inside our published tier set. If a price exists, it is published; if a price is unpublished (e.g., custom institutional pricing post-Phase 5), the offer is *not* in the published tier set.

### Principle 3 — Time-bounded, non-stackable

Founder-cohort has a 60-day sign-up window and locks through one renewal cycle. Annual discount is ~17%. Promotional discounts max 25% / 30 days. **None stack.** Per §6.7 + §6.10 Flag 1 — no "lifetime," no "forever," no "founder discount locked in." If a customer asks "can I keep this rate forever?" the honest answer is no, and we say so.

### Principle 4 — Self-serve and reversible

Cancel, refund (within 14 days), upgrade, downgrade, add seat, remove seat, switch billing cadence — all self-serve. Pricing decisions are reversible by the user. The product never holds the user hostage to pricing through opaque billing or destructive cancellation. Per `_packaging/05-packaging-friction-review.md` design rules 5 + 6.

### Principle 5 — No race to the bottom

We do not match competitor discounts. We do not run "Black Friday" or seasonal price drops. We do not pay influencers in free tiers. We do not run referral programs at v1. Discounts train customers to wait; we want customers to buy because the product fits, not because the discount window closes tonight. Per `_packaging/05-packaging-friction-review.md` design rules 4 + 8.

### Principle 6 — Pricing changes are exit-memo-driven

Prices change only after data justifies the change. The data sources are (in order of priority): P0 cohort exit memo, validation interviews per `[RESEARCH] PRICING — Price Validation Interview Script`, §13 KPI conversion benchmarks, post-launch retention cohorts. Opinions, gut-feel, or competitor pricing changes are insufficient triggers. Per §1 §6 v1 lock posture: prices hold for ≥6 months after validation.

### Principle 7 — The pricing page is a trust-load surface

Per §6.10 anti-overclaim audit — every claim on the pricing page traces to a §3 WTP anchor or a §5 capability maturity. Validation phase status is visible on every paid tier card. AED display is courtesy, billing is USD, jurisdiction is UAE sole prop. The §12 risk register and PCC v2 §8 cap reference are footer-pinned. The pricing page is the single highest-leverage trust surface; it is treated as such.

## 3. Things we explicitly will not do

These are anti-patterns that some category competitors use successfully. We document them here so they are durably out of scope.

| Anti-pattern | Why category does it | Why we don't |
|---|---|---|
| Lifetime deals | Front-loads cash, juices launch metrics | Cannibalizes future revenue; produces legacy-customer friction at every change. §5.3.3 + §6.10 Flag 1. |
| Auto-converting trials with card on file | Drives trial → paid conversion via inertia | Surprise charges erode trust; Free Scope B is the substitute. **Pr2-3** default. |
| "Most popular" / "Best value" pricing-page badges | Increases conversion to higher tier | Pressure mechanics violate Principle 4 + BRAND anti-pressure voice. |
| Hidden enterprise pricing (call sales) | Maximizes price discrimination at top of market | Visibility matches accessibility (PACKAGING gating rule). DF v2 publishes its number. |
| Strikethrough discount anchoring | Manufactures urgency | Manufactured urgency violates Principle 1 (we don't want adverse-selection buyers). |
| Influencer / affiliate referral programs | Cheap acquisition | Pays for adverse selection; anti-ICP risk; Principle 5. Re-evaluate post-Phase 3 only with persona-fit screen. |
| Co-marketing with signal groups, copy-trade, or leverage maximizers | Cross-pollinates audiences | Endorsement-by-association violates §5.3.3 anti-ICP bundling rule. |
| Per-trade fees | Aligns "revenue with usage" | Adverse incentive — we'd be paid more when users trade more, which is anti-capital-preservation. §6.3 explicit reject. |
| Discount stacking (referral + seasonal + annual) | Drives volume on promotion | Race-to-bottom signal; trains negotiation; Principle 5. |
| "Founding member rate locked in" (implicit lifetime) | Hooks early adopters with permanence promise | Violates §6.10 Flag 1 explicitly; canonical phrasing is "locked through your first renewal cycle." |
| Region-blocked content discovered post-onboarding | Captures TAM ceiling | Wastes user time; trust-load violation. Region check at signup per `_packaging/05-packaging-friction-review.md` design rule 7. |

## 5. Pricing-decision audit checklist

Any pricing decision (Phase 2 forward) is audited against:

1. **Honest?** Every claim traces to §3 WTP or §5 capability. No overclaim.
2. **Predictable?** Single number, no surprise charges, pre-notified, self-serve reversible.
3. **Value-tied?** Price reflects what the tier actually delivers at its current maturity (e.g., "stabilizing in cohort" surfaced on Trader card per §6.10 Flag 2).
4. **Anti-pressure?** No urgency mechanics, no badges, no countdown timers, no manufactured discount theater.
5. **Anti-stack?** Discount cannot stack with founder-cohort or annual.
6. **Cohort-driven?** Change driven by P0 exit memo, validation interviews, or §13 KPIs — not opinion.
7. **Anti-overclaim re-audited?** §6.10 flags 1 / 2 / 3 cleared on the change.
8. **Pre-mortem run?** For non-trivial changes, per memory `feedback_premortem_required`.

If any answer is "no," the decision is held until the answer is "yes" or the principle is explicitly amended in this document with founder sign-off.

## 6. When this philosophy gets re-examined

This philosophy is intended as durable through Phase 5. Re-examination triggers:

- Phase 5 Desk Full v2 launch may surface institutional buyer expectations that strain Principle 2 (custom institutional pricing).
- Significant venue expansion (Phase 5+) may change the unit-economics enough to warrant re-pricing the full Track B.
- Macro shifts in crypto regulation may force compliance-driven changes to the pricing or jurisdiction model (per §6.8 + memory `project_jurisdictional`).
- A second product surface (e.g., API-as-product per `_packaging` LATER `API/Data Product Packaging Concept`) may require its own philosophy addendum.

Until any of these fires, the seven principles hold without amendment.
